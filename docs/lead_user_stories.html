2. Epics & User Stories

US-1.1: Single Manual Lead Creation with Branch Scoping
Must Have PER-4 / PER-3
As a Sales Executive or Manager,
I want to quickly capture a new lead with company name, contact person, mobile number, branch, and product interest,
So that the lead is recorded in the CRM and tracked in the sales pipeline without losing inbound prospects.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-1.1.1 (Required Fields): On /leads/create, Company Name, Contact Person, Branch, and Mobile Number are mandatory.
  • AC-1.1.2 (Phone Validation): Enforces international E.164 phone regex /^\+?[0-9]{7,15}$/. Non-numeric or invalid input yields inline error.
  • AC-1.1.3 (Auto-Attributes): System defaults created_by = auth()->id(), company_id = auth()->user()->company_id, and lead_date = today().
  • AC-1.1.4 (Feedback): On submission, redirects to /leads/{id} with toast: "Lead created successfully".
Technical Mapping: LeadController@store | StoreLeadRequest | Model: Lead
US-1.2: Bulk Lead Ingestion via CSV / Excel
Should Have PER-1 / PER-2
As a Branch Manager or Administrator,
I want to upload a spreadsheet of leads and map its columns to CRM fields,
So that marketing event attendees and directories can be ingested in bulk without manual typing.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-1.2.1 (Upload & Parsing): Supports .csv and .xlsx up to 10MB; extracts headers and shows column mapping GUI.
  • AC-1.2.2 (Column Mapping): User can map Company Name, Contact Person, Phone, Email, Source, and Branch.
  • AC-1.2.3 (Duplicate Detection): Checks phone numbers against tenant leads; duplicates are skipped and logged in error summary.
  • AC-1.2.4 (Atomic Transactions): Batch inserts processed in chunks of 100 within safe DB transactions.
Technical Mapping: LeadImportController | Model: Lead
US-1.3: Real-Time Facebook Lead Ads Graph API Ingestion
Must Have PER-1 / System
As a Marketing Administrator,
I want inquiries from Meta Lead Gen Ad campaigns to instantly sync into the CRM via Meta Graph API,
So that our sales team can contact ad respondents within minutes of ad submission.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-1.3.1 (Graph API Sync): Cron php artisan app:facebook-lead-integration polls Graph API v19.0 using campaign tokens.
  • AC-1.3.2 (Field Mapping): Extracts full_name, phone_number, email from Meta's field_data JSON array.
  • AC-1.3.3 (Audit Payload): Stores raw payload in leads.facebook_payload and facebook_lead_id.
  • AC-1.3.4 (Round-Robin): Automatically distributes incoming leads round-robin across configured assigned_users.
Technical Mapping: FacebookLeadImporter | FacebookLeadIntegration
US-2.1: Mass / Single Lead Reallocation
Must Have PER-2 / PER-3
As a Team Leader or Branch Manager,
I want to transfer leads between executives (due to employee absence, resignation, or quota rebalancing),
So that customer communication continues without interruption.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-2.1.1 (Single Reassign): Clicking "Reassign" on lead detail opens a modal to choose the target executive.
  • AC-2.1.2 (Bulk Reassign): Checkbox multi-select on /leads triggers bulk reassignment action bar.
  • AC-2.1.3 (Security Guard): Sales executives cannot reassign leads; only Branch Managers and TLs have permission.
Technical Mapping: LeadReallocationController | Model: Lead
US-3.1: Untouched Leads Priority Queue & SLA Tracking
Must Have PER-3 / PER-4
As a Sales Executive,
I want to see an isolated queue of leads that have never received a telecall,
So that I can prioritize fresh inquiries and achieve first contact within the company SLA (< 2 hours).
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-3.1.1 (Query Filter): /leads/untouched lists leads where COUNT(lead_call_updates) == 0 and status not closed.
  • AC-3.1.2 (SLA Badging): Visual badges highlight lead aging (< 2 hrs green, 2-24 hrs amber, > 24 hrs red).
  • AC-3.1.3 (Auto-Removal): Logging the first call immediately removes the lead from the untouched list.
Technical Mapping: LeadController@untouchedIndex | View: pages.leads.untouched
US-3.2: Logging Telecall Outcomes & Follow-up Tasks
Must Have PER-4
As a Sales Executive,
I want to log call outcome notes and schedule follow-up dates in one modal,
So that interaction history is captured and a reminder is automatically scheduled on my calendar.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-3.2.1: Modal captures Outcome, Outcome Subcategory, Call Notes, Next Follow-Up Date & Time.
  • AC-3.2.2 (Auto Reminder): When a future follow-up date is provided, a record in lead_reminders is auto-created.
  • AC-3.2.3 (Timeline Display): Call log is appended chronologically to the 360-degree lead profile timeline.
Technical Mapping: LeadShowController@storeCall | Models: LeadCallUpdate, LeadReminder
US-4.1: Product Line Items, Discounts & Price Approval Workflow
Must Have PER-4 / PER-1
As a Sales Executive negotiating deal values,
I want to add multiple products, apply discounts, and request manager approval when price is below base unit price,
So that accurate commercial deals can be offered while respecting company margin thresholds.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-4.1.1 (Multi-Products): Supports multiple services/products per lead with unit price, qty, discount %, GST %.
  • AC-4.1.2 (Price Exception): If offered unit price is lower than product base rate, requires manager approval request.
  • AC-4.1.3 (Approval Action): Authorized manager can Approve/Reject via web or mobile API; approval updates unit price and recalculates totals.
Technical Mapping: LeadShowController@storeProduct | LeadProductPriceRequestController
US-4.3: Milestone Payments Collection & TDS Accounting
Must Have PER-4 / Accounts
As an Executive collecting customer advances,
I want to record payments with reference numbers, payment mode, receipt file, and TDS deduction details,
So that commercial accounts reconcile accurately with our bank statements.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-4.3.1: Modal captures Gross Amount, Payment Mode (NEFT/UPI/Cheque), Reference UTR, Date, and Voucher Receipt.
  • AC-4.3.2 (TDS Computation): If TDS is deducted, calculates TDS Amount and Net Bank Credit.
  • AC-4.3.3 (Paid Status): When cumulative payments match or exceed total_price, status transitions to paid.
Technical Mapping: LeadShowController@storeProductPayment | Model: LeadProductPayment
US-5.1: Hierarchical Data Visibility & Tenant Security
Must Have Security Engine
As an Enterprise Security Auditor,
I want lead visibility to be strictly bound to tenant and organizational role level,
So that cross-tenant data leakage is impossible and sales reps cannot snoop on colleagues' leads.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-5.1.1 (Multi-Tenancy): Every query is scoped by company_id; cross-company access returns 403 Forbidden.
  • AC-5.1.2 (Role Scopes): Execs see only assigned leads; TLs see team leads; Branch Managers see branch leads; Admins see company leads.
  • AC-5.1.3 (Central Enforcer): All queries route through DataVisibilityService::applyLeadVisibility($query).
Technical Mapping: DataVisibilityService | BelongsToCompany trait
US-6.1: Deal Conversion & Customer Support Team (CST) Handover
Must Have PER-4 / PER-6
As a Sales Executive closing a deal,
I want to convert the lead to "Won" and allocate a Customer Support TL & Executive,
So that post-sales project fulfillment commences immediately without loss of customer requirements.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-6.1.1 (Conversion Event): Status update to "Won" timestamps converted_at and closure_date.
  • AC-6.1.2 (CST Allocation): Prompts allocation of CST TL and Support Executive; triggers handover notification.
  • AC-6.1.3 (CST Notes): CST executive can log onboarding milestones via /leads/{id}/cst-updates.
Technical Mapping: LeadController@updateStatus | LeadController@storeCstUpdate
US-7.1: Dynamic Custom Fields (Custom Form Builder)
Could Have PER-1
As a Company Administrator,
I want to configure custom lead fields for specific branches without running database migrations,
So that industry-specific lead information (e.g. GSTIN, Turnover, Fleet Size) can be captured.
Acceptance Criteria:
  • AC-7.1.1: Admin defines fields with label, type (text, number, select, date), and validation rules.
  • AC-7.1.2: Fields render dynamically on lead create/edit forms.
  • AC-7.1.3: Values are validated and stored in lead_field_values via EAV model.
Technical Mapping: LeadFormFieldController | Models: LeadFormField, LeadFieldValue
